# MVdWPlaceholderAPI

### I got a lot of spam with this plugin in my console

The problem you're having are probably due to the fact that you don't have a plugin related to the developer of the plugin (like FeatherBoard, ArcadeBoard etc.). As a result, you can't use that plugin, and will send you messages in the console, without stopping, until you have one of its plugins enabled.
